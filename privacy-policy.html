Privacy Policy — Deal Finder UAE

Last updated: 7 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Deal Finder UAE mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information. Deal Finder UAE helps users discover supermarket and retailer flyers, deals, coupons, and nearby store offers in the United Arab Emirates. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

DataWhy we collect itRequired?
Email address To create and secure your account and let you sign in (handled by Firebase Authentication). Required to create an account
Display name (optional) To personalise your account and shopping lists. Optional
Approximate & precise location To show deals, flyers, and stores near you. Location is read from your device only when you grant permission. Optional (you can use the App without it)
Shopping lists & saved items To store the lists and items you create in the App. Optional
Push notification token To send you notifications about new flyers and offers (only if you enable notifications). Optional

We do not collect payment information, contacts, photos, or browsing history. The App does not use third-party advertising networks, and it does not send analytics or crash data to any third-party analytics service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and operate the App's core features (browsing flyers, deals, coupons, shopping lists, and price comparison);
  • Show content relevant to your location, when you choose to share it;
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure;
  • Send notifications you have opted in to receive.

3. How Your Information Is Stored and Processed

The App uses Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage) as its backend service provider to store and process your account data and the content you create. Google processes this data on our behalf as a service provider. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). For information on Google's practices, see the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. Information is only processed by Google Firebase as described above, or where required to comply with the law.

5. Location Permission

The App requests location permission only to surface nearby deals and stores. Location is accessed in the foreground while you use the App; the App does not track your location in the background. You can grant or deny this permission at any time in your device settings, and the App remains usable without it (with reduced location-based features).

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access & correction: You can view and edit your account details within the App.
  • Deletion: You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below. We will delete your account data from Firebase within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.
  • Notifications: You can disable push notifications in your device settings at any time.
  • Location: You can revoke location access in your device settings at any time.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. When you request deletion, we remove your personal data from our active systems within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
